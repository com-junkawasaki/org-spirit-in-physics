Assessment date: 2026-08-23

## Result

**Research-program hypothesis score: 80/100**

Decision: **Advance to preregistration, then test**. The program is highly
aligned, novel, potentially important, and now prospectively falsifiable. Its
score remains limited by weak pilot reliability, the absence of direct energetic
measurements and independent complex validation, N=5 complete-case selection,
and incomplete public reproducibility materials.

This is a transparent proxy assessment, not an output from Google's deployed
Co-Scientist service. Co-Scientist normally ranks competing hypotheses through
pairwise scientific debate and an Elo tournament. With only one submitted
program there is no defensible Elo rating or tournament rank.

## Fastest path above 85/100

1. Convert the Stage 2 framework into a timestamped registration with exact
   effect directions, primary channel, endpoints, exclusion rules, intervention,
   and numerical success thresholds.
2. Collect the simulation-powered repeated-measures cohort and held-out data; make
   reliability and out-of-sample prediction the first gate.
3. Add an independent, blinded criterion for personal complexes and test
   incremental validity beyond latency and skin potential used in construction.
4. Add a controlled RHI or other bodily-self perturbation for $H_{\mathrm{phys}}$.
5. Replace the response-cost analogy with at least one calibrated energetic or
   information-thermodynamic observable for $H_{\mathrm{energy}}$.
6. Test $H_{\mathrm{collective}}$ in an independent and cross-cultural sample
   with prespecified semantic structure; do not rely on PC variance alone.
7. Publicly archive versioned code, environment, checksums, participant flow,
   and an ethics-compatible derived table; verify approval and consent records.

## Relation to the historical 82/100

The repository ADR records a historical ``constructive-review score 82/100.''
It is not directly comparable: no scoring rubric or tournament population was
stored, and that review included claims later found to require correction,
including the 945-trial display count, unsupported Procrustes results, and an
overstated reproducibility claim. The present 80/100 is stricter, decomposed,
evidence-linked, and includes the prospective validation framework. The score
can exceed 85 only after the remaining design choices are frozen and the
reproducibility package is public.
