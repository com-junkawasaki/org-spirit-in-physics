Spirit in Physics is the world's first research platform that measures and visualizes human "spirit" as a thermodynamic information quantity through Jungian word association experiments.

ABOUT THE RESEARCH
This app is a collaborative research project with the Brain Research Institute at Niigata University. We aim to shift "spirit" from a metaphysical concept to a physically measurable quantity.

KEY FEATURES
- Word Association Test (two sessions of 100 words)
- Real-time speech recognition for response recording
- Emotion data measurement from facial expressions and voice
- 3D visualization of spirit structure as a high-dimensional manifold
- Access to research papers

EXPERIMENT FLOW
1. Sign the research consent form
2. Enter demographic information (optional)
3. Set up camera and microphone
4. Complete the word association test (~20 minutes)
5. View your results visualization

DATA PRIVACY
- All data is anonymized and strictly protected
- Compliant with ICH-GCP standards
- Approved by Niigata University Ethics Committee (2024-0269)

REQUIRED PERMISSIONS
- Camera: For facial expression measurement
- Microphone: For recording voice responses

Participation is completely voluntary. Your data contributes to advancing the physics of consciousness and spirit research.
