This is a research application for a collaborative study between the developer and Niigata University Brain Research Institute in Japan.

The app conducts Jungian word association experiments to measure psychological responses. Users see stimulus words and respond verbally with the first word that comes to mind.

To test the app:
1. Open the app and tap "Start Experiment"
2. Sign in with email (you will receive a verification code)
3. Review and accept the research consent
4. Allow camera and microphone access when prompted
5. Complete the word association test (100 words per session)

The app requires camera access for facial expression measurement and microphone access for voice response recording.

This research has been approved by Niigata University Ethics Committee (Approval number: 2024-0269).
