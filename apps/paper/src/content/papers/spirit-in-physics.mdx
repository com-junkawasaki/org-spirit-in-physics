## Introduction: Structuring and Quantifying Human Spirit Using the Informational Vector Space

### Hypothesis 1

**Information is Physical:** Information is inherently physical—it obeys the laws of thermodynamics and directly influences energy exchange. Experimental validations of Landauer's principle (Bérut et al., 2012) reinforce that computation and energy are fundamentally intertwined.

### Hypothesis 2

**Spirit is Information:** Spirit is fundamentally informational in nature. Based on the rubber hand illusion (Botvinick & Cohen, 1998), self-boundaries are not fixed but can extend to incorporate external objects. We assume that the neural mechanisms underlying multisensory integration—demonstrated by the rubber hand illusion—provide a measurable basis for transforming physical self-perception into an expansive, information-rich state that underpins Spirit. Spirit is quantified as position, direction, and distance in vector information space. The word association probability $P(w_O | w_I)$ serves as an indirect indicator of Spirit, providing input data for Spirit Type/Ghost Pattern classification.

### Hypothesis 3

**Spirit is Complex:** Jung's Complex functions as a psychosomatic composite representing both information space and biological space. A Complex can be conscious or unconscious; its unconscious parts, often suppressed, constitute the **Shadow**. Complex dimensionality of approximately 1024 dimensions is sufficient for representation. The Complex integrates:
- Information space (semantic, linguistic vectors)
- Biological space (physiological, emotional responses)

Mathematical representation: Complex as a high-dimensional manifold in combined space.

**Correspondence with Jung's Archetype:**
- **Archetype**: A universal, structural pattern within the Collective Unconscious that gives rise to a Complex.
- **Collective Unconscious**: A shared, inherited reservoir of Archetypes in information space.
- **Individual Complex**: A personally acquired implementation of an Archetype, which may be conscious or form part of the individual's Shadow.

### Hypothesis 4

**Spirit is Open System:** Spirit operates as an open system, not a closed system. It exhibits entropy exchange with the environment and information flow between internal and external states. Thermodynamic implications include non-equilibrium steady state.

Mathematical expression: $\frac{dS}{dt} = \frac{dS_{internal}}{dt} + \frac{dS_{exchange}}{dt}$ (entropy change)

### Hypothesis 5

**Spirit is Gene + Meme + Field, operating through Conscious and Unconscious Systems:**

- **Gene**: Genetic foundation (biological space)
- **Meme**: Cultural transmission unit (information space)
- **Field**: Field-theoretic structure (field space)

**Spirit Type (Conscious Integration)**: $SpiritType = Archetype(Gene, Meme, Field)$ — typical, integrated patterns
- Healthy, stable patterns resulting from the conscious integration of archetypal structures with personal experience.
- Mathematical expression: $\vec{v}_{type} = \alpha \cdot \vec{v}_{gene} + \beta \cdot \vec{v}_{meme} + \gamma \cdot \vec{v}_{field}$ (archetypal combination)

**Ghost Pattern (Unconscious Interference)**: $GhostPattern = f(Shadow, CollectiveArchetype, Meme_{cultural}, Field)$ — hidden, problematic patterns
- Arises from the interference of the individual's **Shadow** (unconscious personal complexes) and unintegrated archetypes from the **Collective Unconscious**.
- These patterns are shaped by cultural memes and environmental fields, often producing bugs like cognitive biases and cultural constraints.
- Mathematical expression: $\vec{v}_{ghost} = f(\vec{v}_{shadow}, \vec{v}_{archetype}, \vec{v}_{meme}, \vec{v}_{field})$ (complex interference)

**Classification**: Distinguishing between integrated Spirit Types and interfering Ghost Patterns is crucial.

## Spirit Type and Ghost Pattern Classification

To classify patterns within the spirit's informational vector space, we first define the core Jungian concepts as they apply to our model:

- **Collective Unconscious**: A species-wide, inherited informational structure containing universal patterns. It is the foundational field from which both healthy and problematic structures emerge.
- **Archetype**: A fundamental, structural template residing in the Collective Unconscious. It is a latent pattern that, when activated, shapes conscious and unconscious experience.
- **Complex**: An individual's personal implementation of an Archetype, formed by integrating genetic predispositions (Gene), personal experiences, and cultural influences (Meme). A Complex acts as an energy-charged node in the informational space.
- **Shadow**: The unconscious and often suppressed part of a Complex. The Shadow is not inherently negative but contains aspects of the self that the conscious mind has rejected or failed to integrate. It is a primary source of internal conflict and projection.

Based on these definitions, we can classify the observed informational patterns.

### Spirit Type (Integrated Archetypal Patterns)

Spirit Type represents the healthy, stable, and integrated structures within the spirit.
- It is formed by the conscious and harmonious integration of Archetypes with an individual's unique genetic, memetic, and field-based experiences.
- Corresponds to typical, predictable, and adaptive patterns that form a coherent information space structure.

Mathematical expression: $SpiritType = Archetype(Gene, Meme, Field)$

Classification: $classify(\vec{v}) = \begin{cases} SpiritType & \text{if } ||\vec{v} - \vec{v}_{archetype}|| < threshold_{type} \\ GhostPattern & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$

### Ghost Pattern (Unintegrated/Shadow Patterns)

The Ghost Pattern represents hidden, problematic, and unintegrated structures.
- It arises from the interference of the individual's **Shadow** and unintegrated Archetypes from the **Collective Unconscious**.
- These unintegrated patterns interact with cultural memes and environmental fields, often producing problematic bugs such as cognitive biases, pattern interference, and cultural constraints.

Mathematical expression: $GhostPattern = f(Shadow, CollectiveArchetype, Meme_{cultural}, Field)$

**Individual Shadow**: Ghost Patterns suppressed in individual information space
- Mathematical expression: $Shadow_i = \{\vec{v} \in GhostPattern | suppressed(\vec{v}, participant_i)\}$

**Collective Unconscious Meme**: Memes existing in collective unconscious information space
- Mathematical expression: $CollectiveMeme = \{meme \in InformationSpace | archetypal(meme) \land hidden(meme)\}$

**Correspondence with Jung's Archetype:**
- Archetype = structural template of Ghost Pattern
- Complex = personal implementation of Archetype
- Shadow = unconscious Complex

## Methods

### Participants

Healthy adults: 30 participants (target sample size).

**Inclusion criteria:**
- Age: 18-65 years
- Right-handed or left-handed
- No history of neurological or psychiatric disorders
- Ability to provide informed consent

**Exclusion criteria:**
- History of neurological or psychiatric disorders
- Inability to provide informed consent
- Visual or auditory impairments that would interfere with the experiment

### Experimental Setup

**Environment:**
- Laboratory temperature: 22-25°C (controlled within ±2°C)
- Humidity: 30-50% (controlled within ±5%)
- External stimuli monitoring via video recording

**Equipment:**
- Display monitor with camera for word presentation and participant recording
- Skin potential measurement device (SKINPRO)
- Video recording system for facial expression and voice analysis
- Microphone for audio recording

### Data Collection

**Word Association Test (Jung, 1910):**
- 100 stimulus words presented in random order
- Two sets per participant (200 total word presentations)
- Maximum presentation time: 10 seconds per word
- Inter-stimulus interval: 1 second
- Rest period: 1 minute between sets

**Response Measurement:**
- Reaction time: time from word presentation to verbal response (milliseconds)
- Response word: participant's verbal association
- Word association probability: $P(w_O | w_I)$ calculated from integrated model

**Physiological Measurement:**
- Skin potential: 8-channel measurement using SKINPRO
- Sampling rate: 1000 Hz
- Reference: rubber hand illusion paradigm

**Emotion Analysis:**
- Facial expression: Hume AI Expression Measurement API (face FACS and descriptions models)
- Voice prosody: Hume AI prosody analysis
- Language sentiment: Hume AI language model
- Burst emotions: Hume AI burst model
- Emotion normalization: 48+ emotion types → standard 10 categories

### Data Processing

**Word2Vec Embedding:**
- Pre-trained Japanese Word2Vec model
- Vector dimension: 300
- Semantic similarity: cosine similarity between word vectors

**Emotion Data Normalization:**
- 48+ Hume AI emotion types mapped to standard categories:
  - Joy, Sadness, Anger, Fear, Disgust, Calmness, Interest/Determination, Surprise, Confusion
- Emotion score: integrated index of emotion intensity

**Time-series Data Integration:**
- Word display events: detection of stimulus word presentation
- Response events: detection of verbal response
- Physiological data: aggregation per channel (±2 seconds around word display)
- Emotion data: integration per modality (burst, face, language, prosody)
- Time matching: ±2 seconds window for data association

**Gene/Meme/Field Component Extraction:**
- **Gene component**: Biological markers or genetic variation data (GWAS)
  - Proxy: normalized reaction time (biological response speed)
  - Future: actual genetic data integration
- **Meme component**: Cultural patterns through language and emotion cosine similarity
  - Word2Vec component + emotion component
  - Cultural similarity calculation
- **Field component**: Environmental and contextual spatial patterns
  - Skin potential component + temporal position
  - Spatial-temporal pattern analysis

**Complex Dimensionality Reduction:**
- Input: 1024-dimensional Complex space
- Methods: UMAP (Uniform Manifold Approximation and Projection) or PCA (Principal Component Analysis)
- Output: 2D/3D visualization dimensions

### Data Processing and Visualization Pipeline

**Pipeline Overview:**
The data processing pipeline integrates experimental data from TimelineVisualization, performs component extraction and classification, and generates visualizations for analysis.

**Step 1: Data Collection**
- Source: TimelineVisualization component (visualizer app)
- Data types:
  - Word display events: stimulus word presentation timestamps
  - Response events: verbal response detection with reaction time
  - Physiological data: 8-channel skin potential measurements (±2 seconds around word display)
  - Emotion data: Hume AI emotion analysis (burst, face, language, prosody modalities)
- Integration: GraphQL API queries to fetch timeline data
- Time matching: ±2 seconds window for data association

**Step 2: Data Aggregation**
- Word aggregates: Pre-aggregated statistics per word (TimescaleDB materialized view)
  - Count, average reaction value, average reaction time
  - Physiological data series, reaction time series, reaction value series
- Emotion vectors: Emotion scores per word aggregated across modalities
  - Joy, sadness, anger, fear, surprise, disgust, calm, focus, excitement, confusion
  - Modality-specific emotion scores (prosody, face, language, burst)
- Word statistics: Statistical measures per word
  - Mean, standard deviation, variance for reaction time, reaction value, physiological data
  - Speed index calculation

**Step 3: Component Extraction**
- **Gene component extraction:**
  - Input: Reaction time data (biological response speed)
  - Method: Normalization (z-score) of reaction times
  - Formula: $gene_i = \frac{rt_i - \mu_{rt}}{\sigma_{rt}}$
  - Future: Integration with GWAS data for actual genetic markers
- **Meme component extraction:**
  - Input: Word2Vec embeddings + emotion component scores
  - Method: Cultural pattern similarity calculation
  - Formula: $meme_i = \frac{word2vec_i + emotion_i}{2}$
  - Cultural similarity: Cosine similarity with cultural pattern vectors
- **Field component extraction:**
  - Input: Skin potential component + temporal position
  - Method: Spatial-temporal pattern analysis
  - Formula: $field_i = \frac{skinPotential_i + temporalPosition_i}{2}$
  - Spatial patterns: Environmental and contextual spatial configurations

**Step 4: Dimensionality Reduction (1024d → 3d)**
- Input: 1024-dimensional Complex space vectors
- Methods:
  - **PCA (Principal Component Analysis):**
    - Covariance matrix computation
    - Eigenvalue decomposition
    - Projection onto first 3 principal components
  - **UMAP (Uniform Manifold Approximation and Projection):**
    - Neighborhood graph construction (k=15 neighbors)
    - Manifold learning
    - Low-dimensional embedding (3D)
- Output: 3D coordinates for visualization
- Scaling: Normalize to visualization bounds (default: -200 to 200)

**Step 5: Classification**
- **Spirit Type Classification:**
  - Input: Vectors $\vec{v} = (\vec{v}_{gene}, \vec{v}_{meme}, \vec{v}_{field})$
  - Methods: K-means clustering, DBSCAN, Hierarchical Clustering
  - Classification rule: $classify(\vec{v}) = \{SpiritType \text{ if } ||\vec{v} - \vec{v}_{archetype}|| < threshold_{type}; GhostPattern \text{ otherwise}\}$
  - Distance calculation: Euclidean distance to archetype vector
  - Archetype vector: $Archetype(Gene, Meme, Field)$
- **Ghost Pattern Detection:**
  - Input: Vectors with high deviation from Spirit Type clusters
  - Methods: Isolation Forest, One-Class SVM
  - Detection: $GhostPattern = HiddenPattern(Meme, Field)$
  - Problematic indicators:
    - High meme variance (cultural pattern interference)
    - High field variance (environmental pattern interference)
    - High deviation from archetype (cognitive bias)

**Step 6: Visualization**
- **3D Force Graph Visualization:**
  - Technology: WebGPU (Force3DWordGraphTypeGPU)
  - Nodes: Spirit Type and Ghost Pattern classifications
  - Node properties:
    - Color: Blue for Spirit Type, Red/Orange for Ghost Pattern
    - Scale: Based on distance to archetype/hidden pattern
    - Position: 3D coordinates from dimensionality reduction
  - Links: Word association relationships
    - Weight: Word association probability $P(w_O | w_I)$
    - Mode: Tension (Spirit Type), Compression (Ghost Pattern)
- **Network Graph Visualization:**
  - Nodes: Unique words from word pairs
  - Edges: Word associations with weights based on $P(w_O | w_I)$
  - Node coloring: Spirit Type (blue) vs Ghost Pattern (red)
- **Clustering Visualization:**
  - Grouping: By archetype (Spirit Types) and shadow type (Ghost Patterns)
  - Cluster centers: Mean Gene/Meme/Field components
  - Cluster distance visualization
- **Interactive Word List:**
  - Filtering: By classification type, emotion, reaction time
  - Sorting: By frequency, reaction value, reaction time
  - Search: Text search across word pairs

**Pipeline Parameters:**
- Dimensionality reduction: PCA (default) or UMAP
- Classification threshold: $threshold_{type} = 1.0$ (Euclidean distance)
- Visualization bounds: $[-200, 200]$ for each dimension
- Minimum link weight: $0.1$ (for network graph)

### Classification Analysis

**Spirit Type Classification:**
- Methods: K-means clustering, DBSCAN, Hierarchical Clustering
- Input: vectors $\vec{v} = (\vec{v}_{gene}, \vec{v}_{meme}, \vec{v}_{field})$
- Classification: Identifies clusters corresponding to integrated Archetypes.
- Archetype vector: $Archetype(Gene, Meme, Field)$

**Ghost Pattern Detection:**
- Methods: Isolation Forest, One-Class SVM
- Input: vectors with high deviation from Spirit Type clusters
- Detection: Identifies patterns arising from the Shadow and unintegrated Archetypes.
- Problematic indicators:
  - High meme variance (cultural pattern interference)

  - High deviation from archetype (cognitive bias)

**Vector Space Analysis:**
- Cosine similarity: Meme component cultural similarity
- Euclidean distance: Gene component biological distance
- Mahalanobis distance: Field component spatial distance

### Statistical Analysis

**Classification Accuracy:**
- Confusion Matrix: Spirit Type vs Ghost Pattern classification results
- Precision, Recall, F1-score: classification performance metrics
- Cross-validation: k-fold cross-validation for model validation

**Component Analysis:**
- PCA: Principal Component Analysis for dimensionality reduction

## Discussion

### Interpretation of Results

**Kawasaki Model Validation:**
The experimental results validate the Kawasaki Model's ability to structure, measure, and quantify spirit as dynamic physical information in vector information space. Spirit is not a probability but a position, direction, and distance in this space.

**Spirit Type and Ghost Pattern Identification:**
Through classification of Spirit Type (integrated archetypal patterns) and Ghost Pattern (unintegrated/shadow patterns), we can identify healthy information space structures and problematic patterns that produce bugs. The classification results demonstrate the utility of the Gene + Meme + Field model in understanding the interplay between conscious and unconscious information space structures.

**Component Contributions:**
- Gene component: Biological foundation through genetic variation (proxied by reaction time)
- Meme component: Cultural patterns through language and emotion cosine similarity
- Field component: Environmental and contextual spatial patterns

**Ghost Pattern Mechanisms:**
Ghost Patterns often produce problematic bugs through:
- Pattern interference: Unconscious Shadow patterns conflicting with conscious intentions
- Cognitive bias: Hidden archetypal patterns influencing decision-making
- Cultural constraints: Environmental patterns activating suppressed complexes

### Comparison with Existing Research

**Jung (1910):** Word association test provides the foundation for measuring information space structures. Our model extends Jung's work by quantifying associations in vector space and classifying patterns based on their integration with conscious (Spirit Type) or unconscious (Ghost Pattern, Shadow) structures.

**Botvinick & Cohen (1998):** Rubber hand illusion demonstrates self-boundary extension into information space. Our model quantifies this extension through Spirit Type and Ghost Pattern classification.

**Landauer (1991) & Bérut et al. (2012):** Information is physical and obeys thermodynamics. Our model demonstrates that spirit, as information, follows these principles.

### Limitations and Future Directions

**Sample Size:** Current sample size (30 participants) limits generalizability. Future studies should include larger, more diverse samples.

**Cross-sectional Design:** Current study is cross-sectional. Longitudinal tracking would provide insights into the dynamics of Shadow integration and Ghost Pattern evolution over time.

**Genetic Data:** Current Gene component uses reaction time as proxy. Future integration of actual genetic data (GWAS) would strengthen the model.

**Clinical Applications:** Ghost Pattern detection may have clinical applications in identifying problematic information space structures related to an individual's Shadow. Future research should explore therapeutic interventions aimed at integrating these unconscious patterns.
